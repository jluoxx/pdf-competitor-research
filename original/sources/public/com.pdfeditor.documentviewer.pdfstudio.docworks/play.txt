About this app
arrow_forward
PDF Editor - Document Viewer is an all-in-one PDF reader, PDF editor and document viewer that helps you open, read, edit and organize important files directly on your phone.
Access PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and text documents easily without switching between multiple apps.
📂 All-in-One Document Reader
Open and view your files anytime:
✨ PDF Reader: Read PDF files smoothly with search, zoom and easy page navigation.
✨ Word Viewer: Open DOC and DOCX documents with a clean and readable layout.
✨ Excel Viewer: View XLS and XLSX spreadsheets, tables and data conveniently.
✨ PowerPoint Viewer: Open PPT and PPTX presentations on your phone.
✨ Text Reader: Read TXT and RTF files quickly.
✍️ PDF Editor & Annotation
Work with PDF documents using practical editing tools:
• Add text, notes and comments to PDF files
• Highlight, underline or draw on PDF pages
• Fill and sign PDF documents
• Rotate, reorder or delete PDF pages
• Merge and split PDF files easily
📷 PDF Scanner & Converter
Turn paper documents and images into PDF files:
• Scan receipts, notes, forms and paper documents
• Convert images and photos to PDF
• Create multi-page PDF files
• Compress PDF files for easier sharing
• Save scanned documents in one place
📁 Smart File Manager
Keep your documents organized:
• Automatically find supported files on your device
• Search documents by file name
• Sort files by name, date or size
• Rename, delete and share documents
• Quickly access recent and favorite files
⚡ Simple, Fast & Convenient
PDF Editor - Document Viewer gives you essential tools to read documents, edit PDFs, scan files and organize office documents in one convenient app.
Perfect for studying, working, reviewing documents, handling office files or managing everyday files on your Android device.
Download PDF Editor - Document Viewer and keep your important documents within easy reach.
Read, edit, scan and manage PDF, Word, Excel and document files.
