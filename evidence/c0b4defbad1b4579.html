Open, read, and manage your files more conveniently with All Document - File Viewer. Designed for smooth and efficient document access, this app helps you view multiple file formats anytime, anywhere.

✨ Key Features
📂 Multi-Format File Support
Open and view popular document formats quickly and conveniently.
📖 Smooth Reading Experience
Enjoy fast loading and stable performance for everyday document viewing.
🔍 Easy File Management
Browse, organize, search, and manage your files with simple controls.
⚡ Quick Access
Open recent and frequently used documents with just one tap.
🎨 Clean & User-Friendly Interface
Simple design for comfortable reading and efficient navigation.
🔐 Privacy Focused
Your files remain on your device with user-controlled access.

• Reading office documents and PDFs
